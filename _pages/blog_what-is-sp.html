What Is SP? How to Earn and Use Game Points

Learn how MEOW SP works, including the signup and daily login rewards, training rewards, and unlocking weapon skins.

SP is MEOW’s in-game point balance. Sign in, earn SP through training, and spend it to unlock weapon skins. The game remains free to play. SP and skins are for use on MEOW only and have no cash value.

How to earn SP

Once you are signed in, you can earn SP in these ways:

ActivityRewardDetails
First-time signup300 SPOnce per account
Link Google100 SPOnce per account; first successful link
Link Discord100 SPOnce per account; first successful link
Daily login30 SPOnce per UTC calendar day; claimed automatically the first time you enter the game that day
Valid training session20 SP per sessionUp to 3 rewarded sessions per day, for a maximum of 60 SP

Training rewards are settled from server-recorded sessions and are not based on your score. A session must meet the validity requirements, and the same session cannot be rewarded twice. Daily training limits reset each day. You can train as a guest, but guest sessions do not earn SP retroactively.

You can link Google and Discord from your profile settings. Each provider gives one 100 SP reward after the account is linked successfully. The reward is recorded once per provider, so unlinking and linking another account cannot be used to claim it again. If you create your MEOW account through Google or Discord, you receive both the signup reward and that provider's link reward.

You can check your SP balance and transaction history from your account after signing in.

What can you use SP for?

SP currently unlocks weapon skins:

  • Common skins: 1,200 SP
  • Rare skins: 2,400 SP
  • Default skin: free

Open the in-game skin shop to preview a skin before buying it. Once purchased, a skin is permanently added to your account and can be equipped or changed at any time. Some skins also change weapon visuals or game sounds.

Skins are cosmetic. They do not improve accuracy, change training difficulty, or affect leaderboard scores.

SP rules

  • You must be signed in to earn, save, or spend SP.
  • Before you buy a skin, the shop shows its price, your current balance, and your balance after purchase.
  • You cannot buy a skin you already own or make a purchase without enough SP.
  • SP can only be used on MEOW. It cannot be withdrawn, transferred, or exchanged for SaaS credits.
  • SP packs are available for direct purchase ($6.99 for 3,800 SP or $14.99 for 8,800 SP), or you can unlock all skins with Membership ($4.99/mo). There is no loot box, wagering, or gambling mechanic.

Start collecting SP

Sign in and enter the game to claim the daily login reward. Earn more by completing training sessions, or top up SP in your account settings or in the skin purchase dialog to instantly unlock the skins you want.
