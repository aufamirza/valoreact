About FEKA HUNT

FEKA HUNT is an homage to classic Duck Hunt style 8-bit arcade shooters. Players take on the role of a hunter aiming at flying GALLINAZO vultures. With energetic Latin-flavored background music, retro chiptune SFX, and witty humor, it delivers instant arcade satisfaction.

Designed for landscape play, FEKA HUNT responds with zero input lag to mouse clicks on PC and touch taps on mobile or tablet browsers. It serves as both a delightful retro game and a dynamic warm-up drill for FPS players.

Controls & Input

  • Mouse Cursor / Touch: Move reticle
  • Left Click / Tap: Fire shotgun (costs 1 bullet)
  • Pause Button ⏸ / ▶: Pause or resume game
  • Audio Button 🔊 / 🔇: Mute or unmute audio

FPS Skill Transfer & Training Benefits

Trajectory Reading & Micro-Flicks

Vultures undulate along sine curves. Instead of trailing targets reactively, train your visual cortex to intercept apexes and linear flight segments.

Trigger Discipline & Ammo Economy

Shots are strictly rationed. Spamming misses leads directly to round failure, reinforcing deliberate crosshair verification before firing.

Target Priority & Fast Switching

Golden vultures yield a massive +50 bonus at high speeds. In Round 3, dual-bird flights force rapid prioritization and snappy target transfers.

Surprise Comedic Endings

FEKA HUNT features two hilarious narrative twists: clearing all 3 rounds prompts the police to arrive and arrest the hunter; running out of ammo triggers a festive dance party celebrating your escape!

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is FEKA HUNT free to play? Do I need to download anything?

Yes, FEKA HUNT is 100% free! It runs entirely in your web browser with HTML5 and Web Audio. No downloads, installations, or plugins required.

Why is there no audio when the game first loads?

Modern web browsers require an initial user click before allowing audio playback. Click anywhere on the game canvas to instantly activate all sound effects and background music.

Does it track and save my high score?

Yes! Your best hunting score is automatically persisted in browser localStorage under 'feka-best'.

How can I get the best experience on mobile or tablet?

Rotate your device into landscape mode and tap the fullscreen button at the top of the player for maximum screen visibility.
